About this deck

Version: Classic HSK 3 cumulative vocabulary (600 cards).

The supplied 2021 PDF contained 603 populated rows, three split parenthetical variants, an alignment error, and unreliable glosses. Variants were merged and meanings were cleaned for study use.

Important: If your test centre confirms HSK 3.0, this deck is not sufficient; use the official new syllabus instead.
