HSK 3 REVIEW CARDS
Offline Windows study app - prepared for the 7 November 2026 exam

QUICK START

1. Extract the ZIP file to a normal folder. Do not run it from inside the ZIP.
2. Double-click Open_HSK3_Review_Cards.html.
3. The app opens in your normal Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome window.
4. On 16 September, begin with the 60-card diagnostic shown on the dashboard.

This Windows 11 package intentionally contains no CMD, EXE, installer, or other
executable launcher, so it does not require a Microsoft Defender SmartScreen
bypass, administrator access, Python, or an account. The app is fully offline.
Keep all extracted files together in the same folder and use the same browser
each time so that your locally stored progress remains available.

each of 20 weekday sessions. Because you took HSK 3 before, 30 is a screening
load, not a demand to memorize 30 completely unfamiliar words. Actively relearn
no more than 15-20 genuine gaps per session.

THE ONE-HOUR ROUTINE (THROUGH 15 OCTOBER)

- 15 minutes: due flashcards
- 20 minutes: today's 30-word screening batch
- 15 minutes: listening or reading, alternating
- 10 minutes: grammar or writing

THE FOUR-HOUR ROUTINE (FROM 16 OCTOBER)

- 60 minutes: vocabulary and spaced review
- 60 minutes: listening
- 60 minutes: reading and grammar
- 60 minutes: writing and error correction

Mock dates: 18 October, 23 October, 28 October, 1 November, and a final mock on
4 November. Use 90 minutes for a test, 70 minutes for careful correction, and
40 minutes each for vocabulary errors and your weakest section. Aim for two
consecutive scores of at least 210/300, with no section below 60/100.

HOW THE APP WORKS

- New words are separate from words already added to your review pool.
- Smart Review prioritizes overdue cards, weak cards, then a random sample.
- Random Practice starts without duplicate words; an Again card returns once.
- Hanzi-to-meaning and meaning-to-Hanzi have separate review schedules.
- Self-grading avoids punishing valid English synonyms.
- Again, Hard, Good, and Easy schedule progressively longer intervals.
- Enter reveals while typing; Space reveals outside the text box; keys 1-4 rate.
- The Hear Word button uses a Chinese voice installed in Windows, if available.

BACKUP AND PRIVACY

Progress is stored only in the browser profile on this Windows computer. It is
not uploaded anywhere. Edge and Chrome may keep separate progress. Use Settings
> Export backup once a week, before changing browsers, moving the app, or
resetting data. The app can restore that JSON backup later.

DECK QUALITY NOTE

The supplied June 2021 PDF is a teacher-made classic HSK 3 list. Although its
title says 600, it contains 603 populated rows because three official combined
entries were split: 哪/哪儿, 那/那儿, and 这/这儿. It also has an 11-row alignment
error and several misleading English glosses. The app merges those variants to
600 cards and uses corrected study meanings. HSK3_clean_600_vocabulary.csv is
included so you can inspect the complete cleaned deck.
